    How Word Rocket Works

    Word Rocket is a sight word practice app built on the first 100 Fry instant words. It uses spaced practice across days to build automatic, on-sight recognition.

    How a session works

    Each session flashes words on cards. Tap ✓ Correct if the student reads it right within 4 seconds, ✗ Incorrect if they miss it, or answer provided if you had to tell them. A miss on a new word starts its rehearsal over.

    Interleaving

    Practice uses incremental rehearsal: a new word is shown, then mixed with one known word, then two, then three, and so on. The known words come up in a random order each round so the student reads the word, not the pattern.

    Spaced repetition

    A word becomes Known after the student reads it correctly within 4 seconds, every time it appears, on 3 practice days in a row. One miss or slow read resets the count. Known words keep showing up as the mix-ins for newer words.

    Word sets

    The app never adds words by itself. The teacher tells the family which words to enter under Choose Words: new words to learn and words the student already knows. Words are listed A to Z.
